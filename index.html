lobby

a card game for hotel guests. there's only one penthouse room left and you're all racing up from the lobby to get it. card numbers are floors.

2 to 4 players
52 card deck, no jokers
10 cards each

one penthouse
room left

◆◇◆

01the aim
be the first to play every card in your hand. if the draw deck runs out first, the lowest floor total in hand wins.

02setup
shuffle the deck, take the jokers out and deal 10 each. the rest goes face down in the middle as the draw deck.

03starting the lift
everyone shows their lowest card. lowest floor goes first and starts the shaft with it. on a tie, suits decide: ♦ then ♣ then ♥ then ♠.

04going up
on your turn play one card on top of the shaft. same floor as the top card, or one higher. a 7 goes on a 6 or a 7.

05stuck
can't play? pick up one card from the draw deck and skip your turn.

◆◇◆

2, the lobby
goes on any card. drops the lift back to floor 2, so next up can play a 2 or a 3.

3 to k, guest floors
played in order, same or one up. j is 11, q is 12, k is 13.

a, the penthouse
clears the shaft to a discard pile. you then play any card to start a new one.

first one up gets the key
