a card game for hotel guests. there's only one penthouse room left and you're all racing up from the lobby to get it. card numbers are floors.

it's a bit of a twist on crazy eights. you're still racing to empty your hand, but you climb floors instead of matching suits, and the 2 and the ace are your wild cards.

2 to 4 players
52 card deck, no jokers
10 cards each, 8 with 2 players

one penthouse
room left
(research)

◆◇◆

01the aim
be the first to play every card in your hand. if the draw deck runs out first, the lowest total in hand wins.

02setup
take the jokers out, shuffle and deal 10 each, or 8 each with 2 players. the rest goes face down as the draw deck.

03starting
everyone shows their lowest card and the lowest goes first by playing it. on a tie, suits decide: ♦ then ♣ then ♥ then ♠.

04your turn
play one card, or every copy of one floor together as a set. it has to be the same floor as the top card or up to 2 floors higher, so a 7 goes on a 5, 6 or 7.

05stuck
can't play? pick up one card from the draw deck and skip your turn.

06the end
the game ends the moment someone needs to draw and the deck's empty. add up your hand: ace 1, 2 to 10 face value, j 11, q 12, k 13. lowest total wins, and on a tie, fewest cards wins.

◆◇◆

2, the lobby
goes on anything. drops the lift back to 2, so the next player can play a 2, 3 or 4.

3 to q, guest floors
played in order, same floor or up to 2 higher. j is 11, q is 12.

k, the penthouse
played like a normal floor. clears the shaft, then you play any card or set to start a new one.

a, the fire alarm
counts as 1 and goes on anything. clears the shaft, then you play any card or set to start a new one.

a set of kings or aces only clears once. if the card you restart with is also a king or ace, it clears again. if your clearing card was your last card, you've won.
